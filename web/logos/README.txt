Put the partner logos here, with exactly these names:
  ihsm.png   (IH.SM – Université de Toliara)
  fapbm.png  (FAPBM)
PNG with transparent background, ~200 px high, works best.
To add/remove a logo, edit CONFIG.LOGOS at the top of the <script> in index.html.
Until the files are present, the page shows a text placeholder instead.
